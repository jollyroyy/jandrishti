# External portrait asset notes

- Rajiv Gandhi portrait reference: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Rajeev_Gandhi.jpg
- The Wikimedia Commons page identifies the source image as public domain in the United States because it was prepared by a U.S. federal government officer or employee as part of official duties.
- Additional portrait references were retrieved from public image search results for U.T. Khader, Rahul Gandhi, and D.K. Shivakumar and prepared as web assets for the dashboard hero. They should be treated as visual references for this prototype and replaced with approved official media before public launch.

The visual inspection showed that the first Rahul crop still contains a pale background and the D.K. Shivakumar crop contains a light background, so the cleanup pass must use a light-background removal strategy for both instead of relying on the darker threshold.
