# NAMMA 204 Dashboard — Design Direction

## Ground-truth reference
The provided image is the visual specification. The app should closely match its dark constituency operations dashboard: a near-black navy canvas, compact left rail, top context bar, glassy bordered cards, green/orange/red/blue status accents, and a dense but breathable three-column information hierarchy.

## Chosen approach: Civic Night Operations

### Design Movement
Contemporary civic-tech command center with restrained glassmorphism and editorial dashboard typography.

### Core Principles
- **Evidence-first hierarchy:** metrics, statuses, and recent updates are scannable before decoration.
- **Civic warmth in a dark system:** human portraiture, tricolor cues, and green action states soften the operational interface.
- **Asymmetry with purpose:** persistent rail + wide center stage + narrow insights column rather than a centered marketing grid.
- **Trust through clarity:** every action gives immediate visual feedback, with no dead-end controls.

### Color Philosophy
The nearly black blue background reflects night-time city operations and lets live statuses carry meaning. Leaf green signals completion and civic progress; amber signals active work; red is reserved for attention and issue reporting; cobalt blue marks information and momentum. Tricolor details are used as a restrained civic signature, not as decoration.

### Layout Paradigm
A fixed rail anchors identity and navigation. The main workspace uses a 12-column asymmetric split: hero and activity content occupy the visual center while map, representative profile, impact, and weekly pulse stack on the right. On smaller screens, the rail becomes a compact top strip and the cards collapse into a single reading flow.

### Signature Elements
- thin blue-gray card borders with soft internal highlights
- status rings and dot legends for operational state
- subtle Indian tricolor ribbon motif at the bottom of the rail

### Interaction Philosophy
Interactions should feel like operating a calm, trustworthy civic system: buttons depress slightly, menus open from their trigger, active navigation is obvious, and overlays are concise. Every visible button either changes view, opens a panel, or confirms a small action.

### Animation
Use 160–220ms ease-out transitions for hover, focus, and dropdowns. Cards lift by 2px with a low-opacity shadow on hover. Use a 500ms entrance fade/translate for the main workspace, staggered by section. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

### Typography System
Use **Space Grotesk** for headlines and numerical metrics, with **DM Sans** for body copy and labels. Headlines are compact and weighty; supporting text stays small, airy, and high-contrast. Numbers use tabular lining figures where possible.

### Brand Essence
**Namma 204** is a public-facing constituency progress desk for Mangaluru residents who want to see issues, works, and impact in one place. Personality: **grounded, accountable, progressive**.

### Brand Voice
Headlines are direct and collective; CTAs are specific and action-oriented; microcopy is calm and human.

- “Your area. Your voice. Our commitment.”
- “See what moved this week.”

### Wordmark & Logo
Use a compact circular civic seal with a hand-and-path visual idea, paired with a bold typographic wordmark where **204** is green. The generated seal should be a text-free icon on transparent background so it can work in the rail and as the favicon.

### Signature Brand Color
**Namma Leaf — #19C65B**, an ownable, vivid civic green used for completion, active navigation, and the 204 wordmark accent.
